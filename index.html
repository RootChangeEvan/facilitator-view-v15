Initiatives

What people are starting together

Every initiative someone has told the exchange about, grouped by what it needs next. Some need a conversation before any introduction would help; others are ready for one. Each shows how firmly people have signed on and whether it has gone quiet.

Stretched thin

People who lead, or are committed to or consulted on, three or more initiatives. They may be carrying a lot. Check in before adding another ask.

Who to introduce

Introductions worth making

Three ways to find them: pairs whose requests and offers line up, requests nobody on the exchange can meet yet, and people with few ties so far. Nothing here is logged automatically; start an introduction when you decide to make one.

Suggested pairs

Requests with no good match yet

Nothing posted on the exchange lines up well with these. Each is a chance for an introduction to someone you know, on or off the exchange, or a sign of something the community doesn't have yet. Matching compares words, so it misses some real fits; your own knowledge of people matters most here.

Least connected

People with the fewest relationships on the map. They may be new, or their relationships may not be recorded yet. The map isn't the whole network, so treat this as a reason to reach out, never as a finding that someone is isolated.

Introductions

Introductions you've made

Record each introduction, whether it came from the suggestions above or from what you know about people. Then mark what came of it. Logged introductions show on the map as dashed lines and drop out of the suggestions.

Commitments

Who is helping whom

Every "I can help" and "I'm interested" made on the community exchange. This is the clearest sign the network is doing something, not just existing. Each one shows on the map as a solid teal line.

Roster

Everyone on the exchange

Click a column to sort, or a name to open their profile. Bridging is high for people whose relationships link parts of the network that would otherwise be far apart. Ties in own circle is the share of someone's relationships that stay inside their own circle.
